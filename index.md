# Numble Support

Welcome to Numble.

Numble is a number puzzle game with two modes: Blitz mode and the Daily Challenge.

---

## Game modes

### Blitz Mode
Blitz mode is fast and forgiving.
- Race against the clock
- Wrong moves reduce time, not the whole game
- Solve as many targets as you can before time runs out

### Daily Challenge
The Daily Challenge is shared by everyone.
- One puzzle per day
- Everyone gets the same numbers
- One wrong move ends the run
- The goal is to build the longest streak

---

## Frequently asked questions

### When does the Daily Challenge reset?
The Daily Challenge resets once per day based on your device local date.

### The Daily Challenge did not change today
- Check your device date and time
- Make sure "Set Automatically" is enabled
- Fully close and reopen the app

### Sound is not playing
- Check Silent Mode on your device
- Raise media volume
- Numble audio is intentionally subtle
